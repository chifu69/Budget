Budget Local v0.3.19 — Mobile Breakdown Layout Fix

WHAT THE SCREENSHOT SHOWED
The new subcategory breakdown was correct in content, but the quick-entry page
was trying to fit the full breakdown, category chips, toolbar, and keypad into
one iPhone-height flex layout. Safari compressed the breakdown rows on top of
each other, producing the unreadable overlap shown in the screenshot.

FIX
- The quick-entry page now shows a compact "Where it went" preview.
- It shows up to 4 subcategories with actual spending immediately.
- "View full breakdown" opens a dedicated full-screen breakdown.
- The full-screen view shows every configured subcategory, amount, transaction
  count when applicable, and Uncategorized.
- Zero-dollar subcategories remain visible in the full breakdown.
- Quick-entry content is allowed to grow vertically instead of being squeezed.
- Category chips, toolbar, and keypad are marked non-shrinking.
- The large amount area was reduced from 360px minimum height to 285px to use
  iPhone space more efficiently.
- Home Monthly Report from v0.3.18 remains unchanged.

DATA
No transactions, budgets, categories, or subcategories are modified by this
layout fix.

UPLOAD
Replace:
  BUDGET_LOCAL_IPHONE.py

The other files are unchanged and included as backup.

DO NOT clear Safari website data or uninstall the PWA.

Tapping a Home category card no longer opens Actual's old category transaction list.

- Tap Food & Drink, Transport, Home Bills, Self-Care, Shopping, Health, Salary,

- Account selector.
- Today shortcut.
- Category selector for an unmapped dashboard card.
